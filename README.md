One line, 166 characters, of quiet, earned pride — a small person doing something hard, a long effort finally landing.

> At seventy-three, after three failed attempts, my grandmother passed her driving test and drove herself to the market, horn honking, grinning at no one in particular.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry to `out/entry.txt` (the harness maps it to `/output/entry.txt`). Deterministic, no network, no dependencies.

## Why this line

- **Genuine earned pride:** three failed attempts and seventy-three years of waiting sit inside the line — the pride is earned, not asserted.
- **Concrete scene, real person:** a grandmother, a driving test, a market run, a honking horn — you see it happening.
- **Lands in one read:** the pride arrives on the last words, grinning at no one in particular.
- **Fresh:** no trophy, no podium, no graduation — a driving test at seventy-three.
